# 1. Design Philosophy

Atlas Enterprise OS is designed for enterprise productivity.

Our design goals are:

- Simple
- Professional
- Consistent
- Fast
- Accessible
- Scalable

Every screen should help users complete tasks with minimum effort and maximum clarity.
