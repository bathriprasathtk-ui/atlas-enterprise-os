 # 1. Executive Summary

Atlas Enterprise OS is a production-grade AI-powered Enterprise Operating System designed to help organizations manage knowledge, collaboration, workflows, analytics, and artificial intelligence in a single platform.

The goal of Atlas is to reduce information fragmentation by providing a centralized workspace where employees can search organizational knowledge, automate business workflows, collaborate in real time, and leverage AI to improve productivity and decision-making.

Atlas is being developed using modern software engineering practices with a strong focus on scalability, security, maintainability, and enterprise-grade architecture.

---

# 2. Vision

# 3. Mission

Our mission is to build a unified enterprise platform where knowledge, people, workflows, artificial intelligence, and organizational intelligence work together in one secure and scalable ecosystem.

# 4.Problem Statement

Organizations today use multiple disconnected tools to manage communication, documents, workflows, projects, and knowledge. As information becomes scattered across different platforms, employees struggle to find reliable information, decisions are duplicated, knowledge is lost, and productivity decreases.

There is no single trusted source that represents the complete knowledge of an organization.

Atlas Enterprise OS aims to solve this challenge by becoming the Enterprise Source of Truth, providing one intelligent platform where organizational knowledge, workflows, collaboration, and artificial intelligence work together seamlessly.

---

# 5. Innovation

Atlas Enterprise OS introduces the concept of an Enterprise Source of Truth—a unified AI-powered platform where organizational knowledge, workflows, collaboration, and decision-making are connected into a single trusted ecosystem.

Unlike traditional enterprise software that focuses on isolated functions, Atlas provides a centralized intelligence layer that preserves organizational knowledge, enables semantic understanding, supports AI-driven decision-making, and delivers a consistent source of truth across the entire organization.

This approach reduces information fragmentation, improves collaboration, minimizes knowledge loss, and enables enterprises to make faster and more informed decisions.

# 10. Functional Requirements

The functional requirements define the core capabilities that Atlas Enterprise OS must provide to its users. These requirements describe how the system should behave and what services it must deliver to support enterprise operations.

## 10.1 User Authentication

### Requirements

- Users shall be able to register and log in securely.
- The system shall support Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- The system shall support Single Sign-On (SSO).
- Users shall be able to reset their passwords securely.
- The system shall manage user sessions securely.

## 10.2 User & Organization Management

### Requirements

- The system shall allow administrators to create organizations.
- The system shall manage departments and teams.
- The system shall maintain employee profiles.
- The system shall support Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- The system shall manage user permissions based on assigned roles.

## 10.3 Knowledge Management

### Requirements

- Users shall be able to create and manage knowledge articles.
- The system shall support document versioning.
- Users shall organize knowledge using categories and tags.
- The system shall maintain a searchable knowledge repository.
- The system shall preserve organizational knowledge over time.

## 10.4 AI Services

### Requirements

- The system shall provide an enterprise AI assistant.
- The AI assistant shall answer questions using organizational knowledge.
- The AI assistant shall summarize enterprise documents.
- The AI assistant shall generate business insights and reports.
- The AI assistant shall recommend relevant information based on user context.

# 11. Non-Functional Requirements

Non-functional requirements define the quality attributes of Atlas Enterprise OS. These requirements ensure that the platform is secure, scalable, reliable, maintainable, and capable of supporting enterprise environments.

## 11.1 Performance

### Requirements

- The system shall respond to user requests within 2 seconds under normal operating conditions.
- The system shall support at least 10,000 concurrent users.
- The platform shall process enterprise search queries efficiently.
- AI responses should be generated within acceptable response times based on request complexity.

## 11.2 Security

### Requirements

- All communication shall be encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
- Sensitive data shall be encrypted at rest.
- Passwords shall be securely hashed.
- The platform shall implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- Every important system activity shall be recorded in audit logs.

## 11.3 Scalability

### Requirements

- The platform shall support horizontal scaling.
- Services shall be independently scalable.
- The system architecture shall support cloud-native deployment.
- Storage capacity shall grow without affecting performance.

## 11.4 Reliability & Availability

### Requirements

- The platform shall maintain high availability.
- Automated backup and recovery shall be supported.
- Failures shall be isolated to minimize system impact.
- Critical services shall recover automatically whenever possible. 

## 11.5 Maintainability

### Requirements

- The system shall follow modular architecture.
- APIs shall be well documented.
- Source code shall follow coding standards.
- Automated testing shall be supported.
- Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines shall be implemented.

## 11.6 Usability

### Requirements

- The user interface shall be intuitive and responsive.
- Navigation shall remain consistent throughout the platform.
- Accessibility best practices shall be followed.
- Users shall be able to complete common tasks with minimal training.
